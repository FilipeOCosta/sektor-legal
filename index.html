Karting lap timing & telemetry

Find the tenth you're missing.

Sektor turns your karting sessions into lap times, racing lines and corner-by-corner analysis — and lets you race your friends long after you've left the track.

Bring the data from your GoPro, RaceBox or lap timer. Sektor does the rest, on your phone, even with no signal at the circuit.

For iPhone and Android. Free to download.

Import any session

Your data, from the gear you already have.

Open a GoPro video, a RaceBox file or an export from another lap timer, and Sektor finds the laps on its own. Set the start/finish line after the session — right on the racing line you actually drove — and every lap is timed to the hundredth.

  • GoPro
  • RaceBox
  • GPX
  • VBO
  • NMEA

Lap and corner analysis

See exactly where the time goes.

Lay two laps over each other and Sektor shows you, corner by corner, where you braked too early, carried less speed or took a tighter line. Mini-sectors light up purple, green and yellow, and the coach turns it all into three things to try next session.

Race your friends

Settle it with data, not with stories.

Run your own races and championships, with standings worked out for you. Add a friend's session to the race and compare your lap with theirs, line against line — or type in the times from the rental track's sheet when that's all there is.

Track leaderboards

How fast is fast, at your track?

Every official layout has a public leaderboard of best laps, split by kart class. Choose a pilot name and your best lap joins it; remove the name and it's gone. Only the time is ever published — never your telemetry. Leaderboards are free, for everyone.
